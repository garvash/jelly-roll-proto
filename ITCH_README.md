# Jelly Roll Proto

**A "Dual-Hero" Fusion Platformer built for the Pyxel Retro Jam.**

Navigate a dark, moody cavern as a nimble driller accompanied by your loyal, shape-shifting Slime. Manage your "Slime Juice" to fire projectiles or fuse with your companion to become an unstoppable Drill Dive.

## 🌀 The Fusion Loop
This prototype focuses on the unique synergy between player and pet. Your slime isn't just a follower—it's your ammunition, your shield, and your greatest tool.

*   **Slime Companion:** An independent ally that follows your every move.
*   **Juice Resource:** Your slime scales in size based on its "Juice." Use it wisely—running empty leaves you vulnerable.
*   **Drill Dive:** Fuse with your slime mid-air to smash through "soft" blocks and execute devastating combat finishers.
*   **Tactical Combat:** Use Slime Spit to stun enemies from a distance, then close the gap with a high-speed Dash or Drill.

## 🎮 Controls
*   **Move:** Arrow Keys
*   **Jump:** Space
*   **Dash:** X
*   **Slime Spit (Projectile):** Z
*   **Drill Dive:** Down + Space (while in mid-air)
*   **Restart Game:** R (on Game Over/Victory)
*   **Quit:** Q

## 🛠️ Vertical Slice Features
*   **Responsive Physics:** Celeste-inspired movement with coyote time, jump buffering, and wall-sliding.
*   **Living World:** Face off against pacing Snails and ceiling-dwelling Bats with custom AI.
*   **Epic Boss Encounter:** Battle the Giant Mole in a multi-phase fight that tests your mastery of the Fusion loop.
*   **Juice Feedback:** Satisfying hit-stop, screen shake, and visual scaling that makes every impact feel heavy.

---
**Technical Note:** This project is a rapid mechanics prototype developed in Pyxel (128x128 resolution, 16-color palette). It serves as the foundation for a full-scale Metroidvania currently in development.
