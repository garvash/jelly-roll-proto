### Subsection placement (§Drill-Dive Contract)

- **D-01:** **New "Enemy Interaction" subsection slots between "Block-break branch contract" and "Two exit conditions"** inside §Drill-Dive Contract. Reads as a third symmetric per-frame collision rule (soft / CRACKED_V / enemy). Heading level `###` matching siblings. Anchor `enemy-interaction`.

Cheap insurance against future readers thinking the two-exit invariant excludes enemies entirely.
- **D-03:** **Subsection content (3 named rules + cross-references):**
  1. Continue-through rule — drill in flight intersecting an enemy AABB deals damage to the enemy, takes no damage from the enemy, and continues drilling (no exit, no bounce, no hitstop). Same passthrough behavior as soft destructibles.
  2. `DRILL_DAMAGE = 1 per hit` (matches `POGO_DAMAGE`). The "upgrade" relative to pogo is structural, not numeric: drill's 2 px/frame plunge means a multi-tile-tall enemy gets hit ~7 times during a single drill chain, so drill out-damages pogo via repeated-frame contact.
  3. `DRILL_ENEMY_COST` per hit — drains juice, analog to `DRILL_CRACKED_V_COST = 20.0`. Value TBD; Phase 33 picks via panel iteration, **target range 10–20**. Naturally caps drill chains; enemies become "tough destructibles you spend juice to kill."
