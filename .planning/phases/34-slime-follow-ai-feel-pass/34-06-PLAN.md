  <how-to-verify>
    EXACT STEPS for the user (do these in the LDtk app, not in a text editor):

    1. Open `assets/output.ldtk` in the LDtk application (https://ldtk.io).

    2. Add a new level with these exact properties:
       - **Identifier**: `Gym_SlimeFollow` (must match exactly — case-sensitive; the warp constant in Plan 07 references this string)
       - **Width**: 80 px (5 tiles × 16 px/tile) minimum — feel free to size up to 160 px (10 tiles) if helpful
       - **Height**: 48 px (3 tiles × 16 px/tile) minimum — up to 96 px (6 tiles) is fine
       - **Background color**: any (cosmetic)
       - **World position**: place adjacent to existing gyms (LDtk will offer a default)

    3. Author the sealed-pocket geometry on the IntGrid layer:
       - Place a 2x2 air pocket in the interior of the level
       - Surround the pocket with solid IntGrid tiles (value `1` — basic solid) on ALL FOUR SIDES (top, bottom, left, right)
       - Place ONE additional solid-tile floor outside the pocket (a "loading" pad) so the player has somewhere to land when warped in OUTSIDE the pocket; player will then teleport into the pocket via Ctrl+0 in S-S2 testing
       - The pocket should be just barely large enough for a 16x16 player — 2x2 interior = 32x32 px = exactly two tiles wide and two tiles tall. The slime will end up "outside" the pocket (where the player came from) and stuck against the wall trying to follow.

    4. Save in LDtk (Ctrl+S in the app). LDtk writes back to `assets/output.ldtk`.

    5. Run the pml-to-ldtk pipeline as you normally do to regenerate `assets/output/simplified/Gym_SlimeFollow/data.json` (this is the format the game loader reads).

    6. Confirm by running:
       ```
       ls assets/output/simplified/Gym_SlimeFollow/
       cat assets/output/simplified/Gym_SlimeFollow/data.json | head -10
       ```
       The directory should exist and the data.json should show `"identifier": "Gym_SlimeFollow"`.
  </how-to-verify>
